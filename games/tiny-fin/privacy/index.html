Privacy Policy

Tiny Fin: Grow Big Fish Game (“the app”), by Ferhat Atagün. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your fish name, progress, pearls, settings and unlocked items are saved locally on your device only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking software, has no in-app purchases and makes no network requests to collect data.

Children

The app is made for children and families. It collects no information from anyone, including children under 13, and contains no links that take children out of the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
