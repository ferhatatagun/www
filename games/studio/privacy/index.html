Privacy Policy

Studyo Solitaire Club (“the app”), by Ferhat Atagün. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Game progress, settings, statistics and daily-puzzle history are saved locally on your device only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking software and makes no network requests to collect data.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
