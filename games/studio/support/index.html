Support

Need help with Studyo Solitaire Club (Solitaire and Daily Sudoku)? Email ferhatatagun2@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We aim to reply within a few days.

Common questions

Is my progress backed up? Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app erases it.

Can I reset my statistics? Yes: open the menu, then Settings, then Reset.

Does the app show ads or collect data? No. See the Privacy Policy.
