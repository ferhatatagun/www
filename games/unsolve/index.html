Unsolve: Reverse Sudoku

Sudoku, backwards. Start from a solved grid and erase numbers while exactly one solution remains. The fewer clues you leave, the better your score. Free, no ads, no data collected.

  • A new grid every day, the same for everyone
  • A streak for every day you reach the minimum, and a daily par to beat
  • Three hints per grid, optional timer, unlimited undo
  • Share your result as a digit-free ■□ pattern, no spoilers
  • Calm paper-and-ink look, soft sounds and haptics
  • English and Turkish, works fully offline
