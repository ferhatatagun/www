Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Summary

The app does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

Your progress, streak, best scores and settings are saved locally on your device only. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking software and has no in-app purchases. The Share button only copies a text pattern to your clipboard or opens the system share sheet, at your request.
