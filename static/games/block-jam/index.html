What it is

Block Jam is an iPhone puzzle game. Slide colored blocks across the grid to the exit gate of the same color, and clear the board before the timer ends. It is coming to the App Store as “Block Jam: Slide Puzzle” and is in TestFlight now.

Try a mini puzzle

Drag a block. Push it into the gate of its own color to send it out. The gate must be at least as wide as the block.

Puzzle 1 of 3Moves: 0

The real game adds a timer, boosters and hundreds of levels.

Features

  • Hundreds of levels on a road map
  • Boosters: a hammer removes one block, a vacuum removes every block of one color, and extra time
  • Crates and locked blocks from level 8
  • Lives, coins and a daily reward
  • A win-streak chest every 5 levels
  • English and Turkish
  • Free with ads, with one optional in-app purchase: Remove Ads
