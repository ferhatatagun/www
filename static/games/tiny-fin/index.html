Tiny Fin: Grow Big Fish Game

A gentle fish-eating-and-growing game for kids and the whole family. Free, no ads, no data collected.

  • One-finger controls, made for small hands
  • 12 levels across 3 ocean worlds
  • Evolve your fish through 3 stages
  • Collect pearls, customise species, colours, flags and eyes
  • Power-ups, storm bonus rounds and a calm mode
  • Plays in 11 languages, works offline
